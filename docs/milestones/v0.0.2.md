# v0.0.2: a small routing increment

Owner: healdropper. Recorded: 2026-09-21.
Status: Accepted planning direction (small increments); routing-first scope is
the planning interpretation of option A. Detailed resource contract and normal
implementation remain gated on discovery and owner acceptance.
[GitHub milestone](https://github.com/healdropper/terraform-provider-goalert/milestone/2)
and [private delivery board](https://github.com/users/healdropper/projects/1).
This milestone is not a release or a production version selection.

## Recorded owner decisions

- The first end-to-end scenario is a Grafana `firing` alert.
- Detecting Grafana itself being down is a desirable later capability, tracked
  separately; it needs an evaluator outside the affected failure domain.
- A Telegram group is the preferred destination to include more people.
  A private group is possible; the concrete group and bot have not been chosen.
- Prefer a small provider increment over bundling the complete end-to-end chain.
- Organize the work through the Foundry SpecDD milestone, issue and Project flow.

These decisions do not authorize creation of a Telegram group, inviting people,
sending messages, modifying production, or silently choosing a bot.

## Planning envelope

Proposed outcome: declare a GoAlert policy connected to an existing managed
service, with ordered steps and one supported webhook action. Prove delivery
to an isolated fake receiver. The Grafana ingress resource and real Telegram
adapter remain later increments; the first product scenario is not a claim
that v0.0.2 alone completes the entire chain.

Reference behavior: [AD-04, AD-05 and AD-07](../specs/alert-delivery.md).
Preserve [SVC-01 through SVC-08](../specs/goalert-service.md).

## Exclusions and unresolved decisions

No incoming-key resource, Telegram transport, actual Grafana rule deployment,
schedule/user management, interactive acknowledgement or outage experiment in
production. Source research has not resolved nested-vs-separate step ownership
or whether runtime webhook limitations permit the intended reliability claim.
The [research](../research/alert-delivery-feasibility.md) remains source-only.

The first `firing` event is accepted as the initial product scenario. Recovery
notifications and acknowledgement remain scope choices for the later end-to-end
milestone. A private group is a proposed privacy choice, not an existing asset.

## Dependencies and completion

Foundation review and discovery precede normal delivery. V002-SPEC precedes
V002-IMPL; verification accompanies implementation and is recorded separately.
Open technical questions are not defects by default; classify this work as
Track C until an accepted contract establishes otherwise.

The [Sprint 1 record](../sprints/sprint-1.md) separates ready planning/discovery
from gated implementation. No dates or effort estimates are committed. The
previous 20% defect-buffer suggestion remains a proposal for owner review.
Issue closure, board Done, merge, release and production adoption are distinct.
Never move an issue to Done merely to close it through Project automation.
